## Adaptation

Default Claude badge: original 18×12 pixel adaptation referencing the silver
frame and dual-fan face of the GeForce RTX 2080 Ti Founders Edition. A 4090 FE
X-frame / single-visible-fan candidate remains in the prototype. These are optical
adaptations, not official sprites, dimensional drawings or model-identical renders.
Official design references:
https://www.nvidia.com/content/nvidiaGDC/zz/en_ZZ/geforce/graphics-cards/rtx-2080-ti.html

The second footer line is editorial; the third lists product names.

## Motion refinement

Intro uses deterministic 4×4-cell binary rain with staggered write heads and
a single-colour logo revealed behind them. The fully revealed frame skips
redundant column clipping to preserve identical software-rasterized edge pixels.
Idle transforms the solid logo into an eye constructed entirely from binary
0/1 glyphs, then reassembles the exact static artwork. At full decode there is
no solid green backing; spaces between the digits are transparent. A deterministic
column flow changes glyph identity and brightness. The original outer boundary
and pupil holes constrain the effect. Both endpoints match static art exactly.
All motion is original project work, not an official animation.

Standalone English title: Inter 4.1 Medium (optical size 32), SIL OFL 1.1.
Outlines are fitted directly to the terminal lattice, as in the Apple theme.
See inter-OFL.txt and the title_font_sha256 field in the theme asset.
