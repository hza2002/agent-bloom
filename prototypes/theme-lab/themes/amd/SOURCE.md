## Adaptation

## Reviewed motion and copy

Idle concentrates energy in the two arrow corners: restrained expansion,
separation and a clipped highlight along each L shape. The white letters do
not receive a red sweep. Intro retains the expanded-core/data-bus construction.

“together we advance” and “high-performance and adaptive computing” derive from
AMD's official strategy release. Capitalization is adapted; the campaign's
trailing underscore is omitted in the terminal caption.
https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2022-6-9-amd-details-strategy-to-drive-next-phase-of-growth.html

https://www.amd.com/en/corporate/amd-story.html
Motion and the red-arrow colour treatment are project adaptations.
