Google 2015 flat-colour identity, preserved as wordmark.svg.
Historical design reference: https://design.google/library/evolving-google-identity

## Adaptation

Small G is optically adapted from the official Google product asset
https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/i/productlogos/googleg/v6/24px.svg
(preserved as `g.svg`). Paired pixel rows and cell-aligned colour boundaries keep the counter and crossbar readable in both quadrant and octant modes; no gradients. Footer copy is editorial, not an official slogan.

## Terminal lettering adaptation

The display wordmark is 174 pixels wide, with 1.14× vertical compensation
centered on the source's 92-pixel height. Source paths, horizontal spacing and
colours are retained. Static, intro and idle use the same adapted letter shapes.
The source SVG and independent mini G remain unchanged.
