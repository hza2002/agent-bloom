## Reviewed direction

The controller (one D-pad and one button group) and tower-console badges are original optical pixel drawings, not official sprites. The saved Xbox outline is mapped to the front of a software-rendered pixel sphere. Interior lighting uses 25 restrained solid green shades; the 256-level experiment was rejected because it overloaded the two-colour terminal cells, with binary silhouette alpha and no edge glow. Awakening/turn and folding/rebound are original review candidates; the previous X-rift design was rejected.
